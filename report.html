The slow arm turns answers into silence

Both arms load the same site in the same minute — a barrier, not two walks that drift apart — and read the same bidder roster off the page. They still disagree about the market, and the disagreement is the link, not the demand.

ru-mobile · silence rate41.0%34 of 83 asked bidders said nothing rather than declining

Measured round-trip time on the mobile arm is 400–1100 ms against a wrapper deadline of 1000 ms. A bidder that answers perfectly promptly still misses the publisher’s own deadline, and the page records silence. So “Russian bidders are less responsive” cannot be read off this arm at all — vantage and channel are perfectly confounded, and the difference is two main effects wearing the interaction’s name. A third arm, the datacentre link degraded to the mobile arm’s measured profile, is what would separate them.

This is not a rounding error in a frame — it is a hole in it, and it falls exactly on the class of site the study is about. An earlier pass of this walk found the vantages agreeing 25 out of 25 and reported no dependence, binding that claim to its rank band; the band was infrastructure and marketplaces. The disagreements begin where the publishers do.

A domain one walk has reached and the other has not is a difference in progress, and is excluded — pooling those would make a slow walk look like a censoring vantage.

What this instrument cannot see

The server-side auction

Everything here is a passive read of what a wrapper hands the browser. Server-to-server bidding never touches it. A domain recorded as carrying no wrapper may be selling its inventory perfectly well.

Any page we did not see

A 401, 403 or 5xx is our own access failing. It is recorded as blindness and never as “this publisher runs no header bidding”.

Wrappers we did not look for

The detector knows Adfox and Prebid, the two the study is about. Google’s and Amazon’s stacks are recorded as evidence but never admit — so a rejection bounds the detector, not the site.

A market

The frame holds a handful of sites, from one or two vantages, on one date, in one six-hour zone of the Moscow clock. The word market is not available yet.
